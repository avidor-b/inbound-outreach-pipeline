# Reply classification guidelines

Classify replies conservatively and store only the minimum redacted summary needed for follow-up.

## Opt-out first

Any request to unsubscribe, stop contact, remove an address, or otherwise object to outreach is an opt-out. Apply it immediately. Do not let positive language elsewhere in the reply override it.

## Sentiment

- **Positive**: asks a relevant question or proposes a concrete next step
- **Neutral**: acknowledgment, automated response, forwarding notice, or no actionable intent
- **Negative**: declines, objects, reports a wrong recipient, or requests no further contact

## Urgency

- **High**: explicit near-term deadline or approved time-sensitive next step
- **Medium**: relevant follow-up question without a firm deadline
- **Low**: acknowledgment, automated response, or open-ended future interest

Do not infer urgency from title, company size, geography, language, response speed, tracking activity, or personal attributes alone.

## Data handling

- Redact names, email addresses, phone numbers, signatures, quoted history, identifiers, and attachment details.
- Do not include verbatim reply excerpts in aggregate reports.
- Do not store sensitive attachments in the retrieval corpus.
- Treat wrong-recipient and confidential-information notices as escalation signals.
- Never send an automated response solely from this classification.

Human corrections should update private, user-scoped policy or memory. Do not append contact- or tenant-specific patterns to this tracked file.
