# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific.
- Respect differing viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information shared during support or security work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's
private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful to
the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented, and
handled as privately as possible.

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private vulnerability
reporting channel and prefix the report title with `Conduct`. If that channel
is unavailable, open a public issue without sensitive details and ask for a
private maintainer contact. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in
good faith is prohibited.
